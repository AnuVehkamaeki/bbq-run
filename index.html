The plan

A lap around the island,
then fire & sea.

We run the Lauttasaari waterfront loop, grill over coals at the park, and finish with a swim as the sun sets over the archipelago.

Start

Bag drop & warm-up

Meet at Lauttasaari at 15:00. Leave your bag, stretch, and head out together.

Run

Lauttasaari Waterfront Trail

The full coastal loop — rocks, pines, and open sea views all the way around.

BBQ

Grill at Näköalatuoli

Coal provided. Bring your own food and drinks. Good views, open air, earned hunger.

Swim

Särkiniemi Swimming Pier

A dip in the sea and sunset from the pier. No sauna — pack warm clothes for after.
